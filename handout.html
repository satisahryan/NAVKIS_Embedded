After today: a 6-step roadmap

  1. Repeat the labs in Wokwi at home — then change one thing in each.
  2. Get a real board: an ESP32 DevKit, a breadboard and a sensor kit cost a few hundred rupees. Install the Arduino IDE with the ESP32 core.
  3. Sharpen your C: pointers, struct, bit operations (|= &= ~ <<), volatile.
  4. Go one level down: ESP-IDF or STM32 HAL, reading datasheets and registers, and a logic analyser.
  5. Learn an RTOS properly (FreeRTOS or Zephyr), then embedded Linux on a Raspberry Pi.
  6. Build and publish one complete project on GitHub, with a README, circuit diagram and video. It is your best interview answer.

Free resources: Wokwi docs and examples · Espressif ESP32 documentation · FreeRTOS.org “Mastering the FreeRTOS Real Time Kernel” · NPTEL courses on embedded systems.
